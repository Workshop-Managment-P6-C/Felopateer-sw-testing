
This checklist covers all mandatory user interface screens required for MVP acceptance, ensuring both English and Arabic (RTL) responsiveness.

---

##  Workshop & Service Screens
- [ ] **Customer & Vehicle Portal:** Registration forms, vehicle search, and job history timeline.
- [ ] **Service Reception & Job Card Creation:** Issue logging, bay assignment, and advisor submission view.
- [ ] **Technician Workstation View:** Kanban/List of assigned job cards, time-logging interface, and part requesting pop-up.
- [ ] **Quality Control & Delivery Dashboard:** Checklist verification screen and delivery status confirmation.

---

## Inventory & Purchasing Screens
- [ ] **Parts Catalog & Stock Overview:** Barcode search, stock level indicators, and reorder alerts.
- [ ] **Purchase Order Management:** Order creation screen, multi-level approval flow, and goods receipt interface.

---

## Training & Certification Screens
- [ ] **Shared Bay Calendar & Scheduler:** Conflict detection modal and training session booking interface.
- [ ] **Student Assessment & Attendance Portal:** Mentor evaluation grid, file/photo evidence uploader, and supervisor sign-off panel.
- [ ] **Student Profile & QR Verification Page:** Student portal for viewing progress and public QR verification view for certificates.

---

## Analytics & Reporting Screens
- [ ] **Executive Dashboard:** Turnaround time, bay utilization, stock health, and training pass/fail KPIs.
- [ ] **Export & Audit Center:** Filtered CSV/PDF export wizard and system audit trail viewer.
